Stay active. Stay motivated. Stay fit.

Kickball Club is a fun and welcoming place where people come together to stay active, make new friends, and enjoy friendly competition. Whether you're new to the game or a seasoned player, everyone is welcome. Join us for great teamwork, lots of laughs, and a chance to be part of a positive and supportive community.

Flexible Fun

Our games are held every Saturday at 10:00 AM. We also have practice sessions on Wednesday evenings at 6:00 PM. Check our schedule section for any updates or changes.
